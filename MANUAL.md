# 🚀 Planet Oops-a-Daisy: How to Play

## The story

Leo was on holiday in space when the ship's computer, **NAV-9000**, took a "scenic shortcut" and crash-landed on
**Planet Oops-a-Daisy**. The crash scattered the ship's parts across four parts of town:

| Zone | Ship part |
|---|---|
| 🏡 Suburbia | ⚡ Spark Plug |
| 🏫 School | 🔋 Hyper-Battery |
| 🛒 Market | 🛞 Steering Wheel |
| 🚀 Spaceport | 🔑 Launch Key |

Find all four and blast off home!

The locals are very friendly, but there's one weird thing about them: **their legs face backwards.** Their knees bend
the wrong way and their toes point behind them. To them, Leo is the strange one: *"Your nose and toes point the SAME
way?!"*

## The big secret 👀

> **Aliens see where they've BEEN, not where they're GOING.**

Because their bodies are on backwards, an alien's eyes look **behind** the direction it's walking.

* The **yellow cone** on the floor shows what an alien can see. Stay out of it!
* **In front of** a walking alien (where it's heading) it can't see you, but it will walk right into you.
* **Behind** a walking alien, you're right in its view.
* When an alien **turns around**, its eyes flip too. Watch for that moment!

## Getting spotted

1. Step into a cone and a **?** bar fills up over the alien's head. It stops and stares at you.
2. Get out of the cone quickly and the bar empties. The alien says "Huh?" and goes back to what it was doing.
3. If the bar fills up all the way: **"HELLO FRIEND!"** A red **!** appears and it runs at you to give you a hug.

### Escaping a chase

* **Turn corners!** Chasing aliens are fast in straight hallways, but their backwards legs get tangled at every corner
  ("SKRRT!" "MY KNEES!"). Twisty paths are your best friend. Long straight hallways are dangerous.
* **Get out of sight.** If they can't see you, they go to where they last saw you, look around and give up.
* They also get **tired** after a while: *"Huff... puff..."*

## Getting hugged 💕

If an alien catches you, it gives you a big squeezy hug: *"I LOVE TOURISTS!"*

* You lose one ❤️ heart.
* If you were holding a key 🗝️, the alien says "Ooh, shiny!" and puts it back where you found it.
* You go back to your last **checkpoint flag** 🚩 (or the start, if you haven't touched one).
* Run out of hearts and you try the level again.

## Your three gadgets

Each gadget needs a few seconds to recharge after you use it. The number on the button counts down.

* **🎺 Squeaky Honk**: aliens right next to you freeze for a moment, confused. Use it to escape a hug!
  But it's LOUD: every alien nearby comes over to see what the noise was.
* **👟 Moonwalk**: put your shoes on backwards and walk like a local. Aliens that look at you just think you're one of
  them (*"Nice backwards shoes!"*). You move slowly, and bumping into someone still counts.
* **🍩 Space Donut**: throw a donut a few steps ahead of you. The nearest hungry alien walks over and munches it.
  Great for luring a guard out of the way.

## Things you'll find

| Thing | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🗝️ Key | Opens a locked orange gate. Walk into the gate while holding it. |
| ⚡ Ship part | Collect every part in the level to win. |
| 🚩 Checkpoint flag | Turns green when you touch it. That's where you come back after a hug. |
| 🟥 Carpet | Super soft: running on it makes no noise. |
| 🟨 Squeaky floor | SQUEAKS even when you tiptoe. Aliens nearby will hear it. |
| ➡️ Conveyor belt | Pushes you along. Walking can't beat it; running barely can. |
| 🕹️ Lever | Flips every conveyor belt the other way. |
| 🔵 Pressure plate | Opens the red laser beams, but only while someone BIG stands on it. Leo is too light! |
| 🖥️ Terminal | Stand still on it for 2 seconds to hack it. Hack them all to drop the blue shield. |
| 🚨 Alarm | In some levels, grabbing the prize sets it off. Everyone comes looking. RUN! |

Noise matters. **Running** makes footstep noise that aliens hear **through walls**. **Walking** is silent.

## Who lives here

| Alien | What it does | How to sneak past |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Stroller** | Walks back and forth | Stay in front of it, slip past when it turns |
| 🟠 **Lookout** | Stands still and looks one way, then the other | Wait until it looks away |
| 💗 **Hugger (Blorp)** | Sleeps... until you make noise or get too close | Tiptoe past, or take another way |
| 🟡 **Recess Kid** | Fast, but can't see very far | One donut gets ALL the kids |
| 🟣 **Heavy** | Big, slow, wears headphones (can't hear you), too big to honk | Lure it with a donut |
| 🔵 **Scooter Rider** | Zooms along a lane, can't see you, can't stop | Wait for a gap and cross |
| 🤖 **Drone** | A robot: it looks FORWARDS, and moonwalking doesn't fool it. If it spots you it BEEPS and calls everyone | Hide behind things and time it |

## Rewards 🎁

* **👕 Wardrobe**: stars unlock new outfits for Leo (a backwards cap, a pineapple helmet, a crown...).
* **🧦 Hidden socks**: every level has one sock hidden in a far-away corner. Can you find all 12?
* **📒 Sticker book**: 17 stickers for funny things, like getting hugged 10 times or never running in School.
* **📸 Share**: after a level, make a picture of your result to show your friends.
* **🎬 Intro**: watch the opening story again from the title screen.

## 🛠️ Make your own levels

Press **🛠️ Editor** on the title screen.

* **Paint** tiles on the left (walls, hedges, gates, carpets, belts...). Tick *Fill a rectangle* to paint big areas fast.
* **Place things**: where Leo starts 🧑‍🚀, ship parts ⚡ (collect them all to win), keys, flags, levers, terminals, a sock.
* **Add aliens**, then pick 👆 *select* and click one to change its kind, name, speed and which way it looks.
  Use 📍 *route* to click the tiles it should patrol between.
* **✅ Checks** on the right tell you if something's wrong ("the ship part can't be reached!").
* **🤖 Robot check**: a robot plays your level. If it wins, your level is fair!
* **▶ Test play** to try it, **💾 Save** to keep it. Saved levels show up in **My levels** on the level screen.
* Made a mistake? **↩️ Undo** (or Ctrl/Cmd + Z).

## Stars ⭐

Each level gives you up to three stars:

* ⭐ **Finish** the level
* ⭐ Finish it **under the par time**
* ⭐ Never get **chased**

Every level is unlocked, so play them in any order. **Easy** gives you more hearts and slower aliens. **Hard** gives
you only two hearts and sharper aliens.

## NAV-9000's top tips

1. *"Walk TOWARDS aliens, not behind them. I calculated this. Probably."*
2. *"Running is fast. Running is also LOUD. I prefer tiptoeing."*
3. *"If something is chasing you, take the twistiest path you can find."*
4. *"Touch every flag. Checkpoints are free!"*
5. *"Donuts solve most problems. This is also true on Earth."*
